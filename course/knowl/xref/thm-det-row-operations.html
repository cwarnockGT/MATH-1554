Theorem 3.10 Row Operations and the Determinant.

Let \(A\) be a square matrix.
🔗
  1. If a multiple of one row of \(A\) is added to another to produce \(B\text{,}\) then \(\operatorname{det}(B)=\operatorname{det}(A)\text{.}\)
    🔗
  2. If two rows of \(A\) are interchanged to produce \(B\text{,}\) then \(\operatorname{det}(B)=-\operatorname{det}(A)\text{.}\)
    🔗
  3. If a row of \(A\) is multiplied by a scalar \(k\) to produce \(B\text{,}\) then \(\operatorname{det}(B)=k\operatorname{det}(A)\text{.}\)
    🔗
